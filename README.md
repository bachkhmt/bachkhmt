## About Me

I'm a third-year Computer Science student at **HCMUT (Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology)**, pursuing an AI engineering track. My focus is on building AI systems that are not just functional, but deeply engineered — from model architecture down to the runtime that executes them.

I work at the intersection of **machine learning, computer vision, and systems programming** — areas I believe need to be understood together to build AI that actually performs in the real world.

**Currently working on:**
- 👁 A real-time facial recognition pipeline with embedding-based identity matching
- 🧩 A custom language parser — lexer, AST, and evaluator built from scratch in C++

**Currently learning:** Machine Learning, Deep Learning  
**Currently reading:** *Designing Machine Learning Systems*

> Open to **internships**, research collaborations, and open-source contributions.
